HOW IT WORKS

Made for the DDNet editor.

By default, images become full-screen backgrounds like Night Themes, centered with 0/0 parallax. High-resolution video frames use exact-size textures; smaller frames are packed into atlases, with one synchronized Color Envelope per frame. STEP curves switch visibility without blending.

Your map includes a spawn and a platform. Open it in the editor to move the media, change the envelopes, or build around it.
